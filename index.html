About S V Healthcare

A legacy of compassion & excellence

Established under the leadership of renowned specialists, S V Healthcare is a multidisciplinary group committed to ethical, technology-driven, patient-centric care. From a single eye hospital founded by Dr. V. K. Nichlani on 15th August 1997, we have grown into four specialty centres across Bhopal.

  • Ethical, evidence-based medicine — honest advice, transparent treatment plans
  • A family of specialists — ophthalmology, pathology, aesthetics and dentistry
  • Modern technology — advanced microscopes, lasers and diagnostics at every centre
