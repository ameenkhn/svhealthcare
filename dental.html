Dental Kingdom

An advanced centre for complete dental care

Dental Kingdom completes the S V Healthcare family — a modern dental centre at Shahpura, Bhopal, led by a prosthodontist and an endodontic-focused dental surgeon, covering everything from routine cleanings to full-mouth rehabilitation.

Inside Dental Kingdom

Comfortable care, modern equipment

Dental Kingdom sits within the S V Healthcare centre at C-222, Shahpura, Bhopal — bringing digital imaging, strict sterilisation protocols and a calm, child-friendly environment to every appointment.

  • Digital dental X-ray — instant on-screen imaging with lower radiation exposure
  • Strict sterilisation — instruments sterilised between every patient
  • Painless dentistry — modern anaesthesia and rotary endodontics
  • Child-friendly clinic — gentle, unhurried first visits for little ones
