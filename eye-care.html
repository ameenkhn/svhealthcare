Eye Care Services

Specialty clinics

Nine dedicated clinics covering every aspect of eye health, for every age.

Comprehensive Eye Examination

Vision assessment, refraction, slit-lamp examination and fundus evaluation — a complete check-up for every patient.

Cataract Clinic

Modern phacoemulsification with topical (injection-free) surgery and premium lenses — monofocal, multifocal, EDOF and toric IOLs.

LASIK & Refractive Clinic

LASIK with customised treatments, PRK, phakic IOL (ICL/IPCL) and clear lens exchange — freedom from glasses, planned scientifically.

Retina Clinic

Diabetic retinopathy, AMD, retinal detachment, vitrectomy, intravitreal injections, retinal lasers and ROP screening.

Glaucoma Clinic

Early diagnosis with OCT and visual fields, followed by medical treatment, laser or surgery to protect your vision.

Cornea Clinic

Corneal transplantation (PKP, DSEK/DSAEK), keratoconus management, corneal ulcers and dry eye treatment.

Pediatric Ophthalmology

Squint correction, lazy eye (amblyopia) therapy, childhood eye disease and myopia control for growing eyes.

Oculoplasty

Eyelid surgery, watering eye treatment, orbital care and cosmetic lid procedures.

Technology

Modern operating theatre, surgical microscopes, lasers and full diagnostic imaging — invested continuously for better outcomes.
