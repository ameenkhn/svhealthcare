Since 15th August 1997

From one eye hospital to a healthcare family

On 15th August 1997, Dr. V. K. Nichlani founded S V Eye Care & Research Centre in Bhopal as a dedicated eye hospital committed to comprehensive ophthalmic care under one roof. At a time when super-specialty eye care was limited, he envisioned a centre equipped with modern technology, specialised expertise and a patient-first approach. Today that vision has grown into S V Healthcare — a multidisciplinary group spanning ophthalmology, diagnostics, medical aesthetics and dentistry, serving thousands of patients every year.

Our Journey

Milestones that built our legacy

1993 – 1997

The Foundation Years

Dr. V. K. Nichlani — MBBS and D.O. from MGM Medical College, Indore, with a fellowship at the renowned Rotary Eye Hospital, Navsari (Gujarat) — serves as Head of Sevasadan Eye Hospital, Bairagarh, Bhopal, building deep experience in comprehensive ophthalmology and microsurgery.

15 August 1997

S V Eye Care & Research Centre is Born

A dedicated eye hospital opens in Bhopal, delivering comprehensive ophthalmic care under one roof — growing into one of the region’s trusted centres for cataract, refractive surgery, glaucoma, retina, cornea, pediatric ophthalmology and oculoplasty.

2000

S V Diagnostics is Established

Dr. Rashmi Nichlani joins the institution and establishes S V Diagnostics. With her expertise in histopathology and cytology, the laboratory expands into advanced pathology — cancer diagnostics, hematology, biochemistry and specialised investigations supporting clinicians with accurate, timely reports.

2019

A New Generation, A Second Branch

Dr. Paresh Nichlani joins S V Eye Care after advanced training in ophthalmology and medical retina — bringing modern cataract surgery, LASIK and refractive expertise, and leading the expansion to the second branch at Shahpura, Bhopal.

2025

Pearl Aesthetics Opens

Dr. Sugandha Nichlani establishes Pearl Aesthetics — a doctor-led aesthetic medicine centre. After her MBBS, she earned her Fellowship in Aesthetic Medicine from ILAMED (Germany) and refined her skills at Kokilaben Dhirubhai Ambani Hospital, Mumbai.

Today

Dental Kingdom Completes the Family

Led by Dr. Garima Nichlani (MDS — Prosthodontics, Mumbai) and Dr. Hiren Meghani (endodontics & restorative dentistry), Dental Kingdom brings comprehensive oral healthcare under one roof — completing a multidisciplinary healthcare family united by ethical practice and personalised care.
